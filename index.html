Clipping Hub

Personal YouTube publishing tool for Luis R

Clipping Hub is a private desktop tool used only by its owner (Luis R) to publish short-form video clips to his own YouTube channel. It is not a public product and is not offered to other users.

What it does

  • Connects to YouTube with Google OAuth so the owner can upload and manage clips on his channel.
  • Sets required YouTube labels (including synthetic-media / AI disclosure) when a clip is published.
  • Runs on the owner’s Mac at a local address (127.0.0.1) and does not provide a public web service for third parties.

Google data

The app requests the YouTube Data API scope needed to upload and manage videos on the owner’s channel. Google account data is used only for that purpose. See the Privacy Policy for details.
